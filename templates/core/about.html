About CourtList

Why we built it

A Euroleague game night involves dozens of small jobs: referee crews to confirm, press passes to approve, shot clocks to test and supplies to order. Meanwhile, fans want to call the result and argue about it.

CourtList brings both sides together. Fans predict every game and discuss it, while our operations staff track their matchday tasks on a Kanban board and restock from our own supply shop.
